# Free USMLE practice questions and study tools (USMLE Explained)

A one-page index of everything free on [USMLE Explained](https://usmleexplained.com): practice questions for Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3 where every answer explains why each wrong option is wrong and links its official source, one-page Step 1 cheat sheets, and free [study tools](https://usmleexplained.com/tools/) (NBME lab values, anion gap, ABG and sensitivity and specificity calculators).
